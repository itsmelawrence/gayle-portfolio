Most businesses hire for these separately. Each one works better when it is built with the others in mind: research shapes the creative, the creative feeds the campaign, and the campaign is only as good as what happens after the click.

About

I work on both sides of marketing: the visible side, like ads, video, and social content, and the operational side that keeps it running.

I like understanding how a business actually works before touching its marketing. That is usually where the useful fixes are: a campaign that needs a clearer structure, a lead journey with gaps in it, or a task someone repeats by hand every week.

From researching competitor ads and structuring lead generation campaigns to editing creatives, building funnels, and automating follow-up, I take projects from plan to launch and stay responsible for the details.

Longer term, I’m building toward a small team that offers the same thing at greater scale: reliable execution and well-structured marketing systems.

  • Independent

    Takes projects from planning to launch without needing step-by-step direction.

  • Adaptable

    Moves between ads, creative, social, and technical setup across different industries.

  • Execution-focused

    Turns plans into live campaigns, creatives, funnels, and workflows.

  • Resourceful

    Uses research and AI tools to solve problems and work more efficiently.

  • Detail-oriented

    Cares about clean campaign structure, tidy workflows, and accurate data.

  • Always learning

    Keeps picking up new tools and sharpening the craft.
